Room appearance is presentation owned by Navet. It does not change the devices assigned to the
room.

![The Room appearance dialog with symbol and room image controls.](/docs/how-to/rooms/room-appearance-dialog.webp)

## Change the room symbol

1. Open the Rooms workspace.
2. Select a room.
3. Choose **Room appearance**.
4. Choose a symbol.

## Choose a room image

Select a built-in image or enter an `http` or `https` image address. Review the preview before
applying it.

External images can fail when the source blocks embedding, requires authentication, or becomes
unavailable.

## Remove an image

Choose **Remove image**, then apply the appearance. The room returns to its theme-native surface.

![Room navigation and the selected room after applying a symbol and image.](/docs/how-to/rooms/room-appearance-result.webp)

## Keep room navigation readable

- Prefer images with a clear center and moderate contrast.
- Avoid text embedded in the image.
- Check the result in light, dark, black, and glass themes if household screens use different
  themes.
- Keep the room name recognizable without relying on the image alone.
