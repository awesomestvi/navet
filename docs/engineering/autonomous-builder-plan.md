## Proactive UX audit proposal

Task-scoped review follows the [UX guide](../../ai/skills/navet-ux.md). A separately authorized
programme may rotate weekly across essential journeys once its runner, resources and private
finding destination are configured. The rotation is independent of the opportunity limit.

- Inspect isolated stories and connected journeys for hierarchy, spacing, density, geometry,
  contrast, touch, focus, keyboard navigation, scroll ownership, overlays, overflow, state and
  save/reopen behavior. Compare shared tokens and the closest component family.
- Record commit, journey/component, viewport, theme, input, state and reviewed/failed/unverified
  coverage. Routine PRs use a risk-based matrix; release candidates use the agreed complete matrix.
- Findings include reproduction, actual/expected behavior, violated reference, artifact, impact,
  confidence and smallest repair. Separate defects, improvement proposals and suspicions;
  consolidate duplicate causes. Prioritize blocked actions, hidden controls, unreadable state,
  lost edits and navigation failures.
- Maintain maintainer-approved screenshot baselines. Triage rendering noise before attributing
  differences to defects. Accessibility and screenshot checks alone cannot establish UX quality.
- Fix confirmed changed-scope defects and record pre-existing owners/dispositions. Track escaped
  defects, reopened findings, false positives, coverage, primitive reuse and maintainer effort.
  The maintainer judges product taste from rendered evidence.

## Proposed 1.0 acceptance

These criteria need maintainer agreement; workflow completion does not establish product readiness.
The roadmap does not require every proposed feature to ship.

1. Define supported deployments, provider capabilities, browsers/devices, persistence guarantees
   and known limitations.
2. Agree a household cohort including non-maintainers and an observation period. Verify setup,
   dashboard creation, daily controls and relevant chores; resolve repeated blocking friction.
3. Verify actual provider capabilities and owning-provider command routing. Record real-runtime
   evidence separately from mocks and fixtures, with honest unsupported-capability behavior.
4. Verify fresh install, upgrades, backup/restore and persisted-data compatibility for Docker,
   Home Assistant add-on and custom panel.
5. Close release-blocking security, authorization, data-loss and reliability findings under an
   agreed severity policy; explicitly disposition remaining issues.
6. Complete responsive/accessibility review of essential journeys, shared components and loading,
   unavailable, error and permission states. Resolve confirmed defects in the agreed matrix and
   obtain maintainer design acceptance of the current candidate.
7. Verify current-head required CI, resolved review conversations, usable previews, coherent user
   docs and explicit limitations.
8. Follow the [release workflow](../release-workflow.md): beta/RC, actual-image verification,
   maintainer installation test, exact-tag stable dispatch and distribution verification.

Each criterion records results, owner and remaining work. The maintainer accepts the tested
candidate and dispatches the release separately.

## Activation and evaluation

Expand autonomy only after separately authorized pilots demonstrate authority handling, durable
ownership/recovery, fresh scope checks, complete measured accounting, usable approval packages
and acceptable correction effort. Preserve the maintainer-controlled pause and unfinished state.

The broader rollout proposal uses five representative authorized tasks, including UI work, a
reproduced bug, documentation and interrupted recovery; exercise one proposal through approval
and one rendered finding through repair and independent retest. Numerical resources and targets
require accepted policy. Retain evidence and remaining criteria in the private Project.

Standing maintenance needs category-specific authority and limits. Research, priority, agent
writes and elapsed time grant no implementation approval. Recurring research, live trials,
private installation access, merging and publishing retain their separate authorization gates.
Coordinated delivery uses one PR or an explicitly accepted ordered stack, with integration owned
by the delivery agent. Use the existing work brief and approval package; avoid competing queues,
contracts or additional general reviewers without measured justification.
