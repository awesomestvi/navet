Control your smart home with Navet on a wall panel, tablet, computer, or phone.
These guides help you install it, build your first dashboard, and make it your own.

## Start here

1. **Install Navet.** [Choose an installation](/install/) for Home Assistant, Homey, or openHAB.
2. **Set up your dashboard.** Follow [Your first 15 minutes](/guide/quick-start/first-15-minutes/)
   to complete setup and add your first card.
3. **Make it yours.** [Customize Home](/guide/dashboards/customize-home/) with the controls
   and information you use most.

Want a little context first? [Getting started](/getting-started/) explains what you need.

## Already using Navet?

- [Browse the how-to guides](/guide/how-to/) to finish a specific task.
- [Explore everyday controls](/guide/everyday-control/) for lights, climate, media, and more.
